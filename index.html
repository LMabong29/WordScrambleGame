How to Play:

  • Start the Game: Enter your name and press "Start" to begin.
  • Unscramble the Word: You are given a scrambled word, and you need to rearrange the letters to form the correct word.
  • Hint: If you're stuck, use the hint provided to get a clue about the word.
  • Timer and Score: Complete the word within the time limit to earn points. The faster you finish, the more points you get.
  • Next Round: Once you correctly unscramble a word, you move on to the next round.
  • Win the Game: Complete all levels and challenge yourself to beat your high score!
  • Restart: Press "Restart" to start over at any time.
